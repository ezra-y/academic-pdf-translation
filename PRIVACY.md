# Privacy

Academic PDF Translation is a local, skills-only plugin. It does not operate a
developer-hosted server, create user accounts, collect telemetry, or send
documents to the plugin author.

The plugin instructs the user's AI agent to process PDF files and generated
artifacts in the user's local workspace. It explicitly prohibits third-party
machine-translation APIs and local small-model services. The AI product or
runtime used to execute the plugin may process prompts and files under that
provider's own privacy terms.

The plugin author does not receive, store, sell, or share personal data through
the plugin.

Questions may be filed at
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Ezra-Y/academic-pdf-translation/issues).
