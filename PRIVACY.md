# Privacy

Academic PDF Translation is a local, skills-only plugin. It does not operate a
developer-hosted server, create user accounts, collect telemetry, or send
documents to the plugin author.

## Data processed

The plugin may process only the files and instructions the user chooses to
provide, including source PDFs, existing translations, translation settings,
and generated review or output files.

## Purpose and recipients

This data is used only to translate, reconstruct, review, and organize the
user's documents. Processing occurs in the user's selected AI product or local
workspace. That AI product or runtime may receive prompts and files under its
own privacy terms. The plugin author does not receive the data, and the plugin
does not send it to a developer-hosted service, advertising network, data
broker, or third-party machine-translation API.

## Retention

The plugin author retains no user data. Local input, process, and output files
remain in the user's workspace until the user deletes them. Any retention by
the selected AI product or runtime is governed by that provider's terms and
account settings.

## User controls

Users choose which files to provide, may stop processing at any time, and may
delete the local workspace and outputs. The plugin has no separate account,
tracking identifier, or author-controlled data store.

The plugin author does not sell or share personal data through the plugin.
Questions may be filed at
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Ezra-Y/academic-pdf-translation/issues).
